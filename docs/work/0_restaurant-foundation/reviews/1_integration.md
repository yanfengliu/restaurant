Keep the accepted action and resulting guest state consistent, either by refusing removal while occupied or by transitioning the occupant coherently, and cover the immediate paused save/load path.

The UI amplifies F2: `src/main.js:44-50` replaces a rejected startup save with a fresh bistro, and `src/main.js:466` autosaves on the first animation frame because `savedAt` begins at zero. A user who saves this validly constructed state and reloads can therefore lose the restaurant and its stored save. At the review cutoff, the integration owner was considering protection of an invalid saved value; no such protection is claimed to exist at this target.

**F3 — P2: The advertised drag gesture does not move the camera.** `src/main.js:55`, `src/main.js:328`, the hover prompt, and the README advertise a plain drag for camera movement. An ordinary left drag takes neither movement branch and suppresses the click when released. The actual panning gesture is therefore undiscoverable from the advertised controls, and ordinary dragging appears broken. Implement the intended plain-drag behavior or expose accurate Shift/middle-drag panning and right-drag orbit instructions, then verify the documented gestures through actual browser inputs.

A nonblocking factual-copy observation was also sent to the integration owner: the help text at `src/main.js:328` claims good reviews bring more guests, although `arrivals` at `src/engine.js:178-192` uses a fixed seeded interval independent of rating.
